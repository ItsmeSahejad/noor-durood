DUROOD SHARIF

Durood Sharif – Meaning, Benefits & Importance

Durood Sharif is a beautiful form of sending blessings and peace upon Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Reciting Durood with sincerity can become a meaningful part of daily remembrance and spiritual reflection.

What is Durood Sharif?

Durood, also known as Salawat, refers to prayers and blessings upon Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Muslims recite Durood as an expression of love, respect and remembrance of the Prophet ﷺ.

Why Recite Durood?

Durood can help a person develop a regular habit of remembrance, reflection and gratitude. Many Muslims include Durood in their daily spiritual routine, especially after Salah and during moments of personal reflection.

How to Recite Durood

There are different authentic forms of Salawat. One well-known form is Durood Ibrahim, which is recited during Salah. You may also make Durood part of your daily routine according to your personal practice.

Durood Ibrahim

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

Make Durood Part of Your Daily Routine

Instead of focusing only on a number, focus on consistency, sincerity and remembrance. A few moments of peaceful Durood every day can become a valuable spiritual habit.
